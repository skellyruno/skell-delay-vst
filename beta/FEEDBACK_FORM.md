# Beta feedback (about 10 minutes, once a week)

1. What did you use it on (voice, guitar, synth...) and in which host?
2. Rate 1-5: sound quality / ease of use / looks / CPU use.
3. Which 3 presets did you use most? Which didn't work for you?
4. Elastic, Telepathy, Pocket, Ecology: for each, did it do something audible? Was it musical? Did it ever sound wrong? Did the little status note under it make sense?
5. Did you find the ADV pages? What did you expect to find that wasn't there?
6. Anything confusing, tiny to read, or hard to click?
7. Did anything glitch, click, go quiet or run away (feedback)? When?
8. What would make you pay for this instead of your current delay? What price feels fair?
9. Anything else?
